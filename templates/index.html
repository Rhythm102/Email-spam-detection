Why Use AI for Spam Detection?

Traditional filters rely on "Blacklists" and "Keywords," but modern cyber-attacks are much smarter. Scammers now use Polymorphic Phishing—changing their message structure for every target to stay invisible.

• Deep Semantic Analysis: Our AI doesn't just look for "bad words." It understands the tone and context of the entire email to identify hidden threats.
• Zero-Day Protection: It can detect brand-new scam patterns that haven't been reported yet by recognizing suspicious behavioral traits.
• Combatting AI-Generated Scams: As hackers use LLMs to write "perfect" emails, only an AI-driven defense can effectively identify the subtle robotic patterns in their text.
