Why Use AI for Spam Detection?

Traditional filters rely on "Blacklists" and "Keywords," but modern cyber-attacks are much smarter. Scammers now use Polymorphic Phishing—changing their message structure for every target to stay invisible.

• Deep Semantic Analysis: Our AI understands the tone and context of the entire email to identify hidden threats.
• Zero-Day Protection: It detects brand-new scam patterns by recognizing suspicious behavioral traits instead of just old data.
• Combatting AI-Generated Scams: As hackers use LLMs to write "perfect" emails, our AI identifies subtle robotic patterns in their text.
